Help

Main screen
  • Private games are for family and friends: Create a game, then send the others the link or the game code. Got a code? Enter it to join.
  • Public games are for playing with other people on GamesLane: Create a game, or Join a game to be seated in a game waiting for players.
  • Your icon and name, at the top, open your profile; the flag changes the language.
More details
  • You start as a guest. Log in or create an account in your profile to keep your profile, game order and game options on any device.
  • In your profile you can change your name and icon, turn the chat and sounds on or off, and choose how the cards look.
  • Drag a game by its ⋮⋮ handle to change the order of the games.
  • If you have a game in progress, the main screen offers to Return to it.
  • A good setup: a shared screen (a TV or laptop) watches the game and shows the table, while each player plays on their own phone.
Getting a game started
  • Tap Sit here to take a seat; tap your seat to leave it. Anyone without a seat watches.
  • Once enough players are seated, any player presses Start.
  • The Game options button shows the rules chosen for the game; tap it to change them.
More details
  • Invite others with the link or the game code (🔑, tap it to copy it). The ☰ menu also has the link, and lets you leave your seat or end the game for everyone.
  • 💬 opens the chat, for everyone in the session.
  • In a public game, only its creator changes the options and starts the game; it starts by itself once all the seats are taken.
  • If you lose your connection, your seat is kept: during a game you can come back at any time. If a player is missing when the game needs them, the others can wait, skip their turn (in some games), or end the game.
  • Saved users can save the options as their preferences for the game.
Tic-tac-toe

How to play

  • 2 players, X and O, take turns placing their mark. Three in a row (across, down or diagonally) wins the round.

On this screen

  • Tap an empty square on your turn. The bar at the top turns gold when it is your turn, and shows the score.
More details
  • Wins and draws are counted round after round. X starts the odd rounds, O the even ones.
  • The game never ends by itself: leave your seat or the game when you are done.
Backgammon

How to play

  • 2 players race their 15 checkers around the board to their home board, then bear them off. The first to bear off all their checkers wins the game.
  • A match is played to a number of points (5 by default).

On this screen

  • Tap the board or 🎲 Roll to roll. Tap a checker, then one of its highlighted points, and press Done.
  • Undo takes back a move; Last move shows the other player's last turn.
More details
  • The game checks the rules: a checker on the bar enters first, a point with two or more opposing checkers is blocked, a single opposing checker is hit, and you must use as many dice as you can.
  • A gammon (the loser bore off no checker) scores 2 points, a backgammon 3, times the doubling cube.
  • Double before rolling to double the stakes; the other player Takes or Drops (losing the game).
  • A checker can move by both dice at once when every way gives the same result. If only one move is possible, the game plays it for you.
The Gang

How to play

  • 3–8 players, playing together. Each heist, everyone gets 2 secret cards, and up to 5 shared cards are dealt.
  • In 4 stages, each player takes a numbered chip: 1 for the weakest poker hand, up to the number of players for the strongest. You cannot tell anyone your cards; the chips are your only way to talk.
  • The hands are revealed from the lowest red chip up. In order, the vault opens; out of order, the alarm goes off. 3 vaults win, 3 alarms lose.

On this screen

  • Tap a chip in the middle or another player's chip to take it; tap your own to put it back. Press Confirm when everyone has a chip.
  • Hide table leaves only the shared cards and your hand, e.g. when a TV shows the table.
More details
  • Hand rankings, from weakest: high card, pair, two pair, three of a kind, straight, flush, full house, four of a kind, straight flush.
  • Any chip change clears everyone's confirmation.
  • Advanced mode adds challenge cards (after a vault opens) and specialist cards (after an alarm). Tap a card to read what it does.
  • With the Jokers option, a player dealt a joker swaps it for one of two cards.
Rich man, poor man

How to play

  • 4–12 players. Get rid of all your cards. Only numbers count: 2 is the lowest and A the highest; suits do not matter.
  • The round's starter throws any number of cards of one number. Each player in turn throws as many cards of a higher number, or passes. When everyone else has passed, the last thrower wins the round and starts the next. Aces win the round at once.
  • The order in which players empty their hands sets the places for the next game: the first is the rich man, the last the poor man.

On this screen

  • Tap cards of one number, then Select to throw them, or Pass. Tapping a card picks as many as are needed.
  • The bar at the top says what to do.
More details
  • Before each later game, the rich give the poor their choice of cards, and the poor give the rich their highest cards (more cards for the players further apart).
  • The poorest player starts each later game. In the first game, the player with the most 2s starts.
  • A player who is away can be skipped. Observers can turn on Read throws aloud.
Wazoo

How to play

  • 1–8 players, 13 rounds. On your turn, roll five dice up to three times, keeping the dice you like between rolls.
  • Then score the dice in one open box of your scorecard. Every box is filled once; the highest total wins.

On this screen

  • Press 🎲 Roll. Tap dice to keep them (tap again to let go). Tap an open box to score there; it shows what the dice would score.
More details
  • Ones to Sixes score the dice of that number; 63 or more in them gives a 35 bonus.
  • Three and Four of a kind score all the dice; full house 25, small straight (4 in a row) 30, large straight (5 in a row) 40, Wazoo (five of a kind) 50, Chance all the dice.
  • Each extra Wazoo, after scoring 50 for one, gives 100 more, and it can score in other boxes (the joker rule).
Final Card

How to play

  • 2–10 players. Be the first to play all your cards. Play a card matching the top of the pile by color or by number or symbol, or a Change Color; otherwise draw.
  • Action cards: Stop skips the next player, Change Direction reverses play, +2 and Change Color +4 make the next player draw.
  • With one card left, press Final Card!, or you can be caught and draw penalty cards.

On this screen

  • Tap a card to play it (the cards you cannot play are greyed). Tap the draw pile or Draw a card to draw.
  • Final Card! is always under your cards: it protects you, and catches anyone else who forgot.
More details
  • The Style option picks US Style (the UNO rules) or Israeli Style (the Super TAKI rules, with TAKI, Plus, Crown and +3 cards).
  • Options also set when the game ends (one game, a score target, or a pyramid), stacking draw cards, and when Change Color +4 can be played or challenged.
  • Penalty cards must be drawn before you do anything else.
  • In a TAKI, play as many cards of its color as you like, then press Close TAKI.
Yaniv

How to play

  • 2–4 players, 5 cards each. Lower your hand score: ace 1, joker 0, 2–10 their number, J, Q and K 10.
  • On your turn, discard one card, a series of the same value, or a straight of 3 or more in one suit; then take a card from the closed pile or the top of the open pile.
  • With 7 points or less, call Yaniv! If nobody is lower, you win the round; otherwise you are Assaf and get 30 extra points.

On this screen

  • Tap the cards to discard, then tap the pile to take a card from. Yaniv! appears next to your hand score when you can call it.
More details
  • A joker can stand for any card in a series or a straight. The ace is low only: A-2-3 is a straight, Q-K-A is not.
  • After a Yaniv call, every other player plays one last turn, then all the hands are shown.
  • With a score target, a player above the target is out; landing exactly on half the target or on the target takes 50 points off.
