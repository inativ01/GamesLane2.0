Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

GamesLane is a free, non-commercial site for playing board and card games with friends and family. This page explains what information it uses and why.

Information we use

  • Your display name and icon, which you choose. They are shown to the other people in your game.
  • If you create an account: your username and a password. The password is stored only in scrambled form (a salted hash), so nobody, including GamesLane, can read it.
  • If you sign in with Google or Microsoft: your account ID at that service, used only to recognise you next time, and your name and profile picture, used once as your default display name and icon. GamesLane never sees your Google or Microsoft password, and does not keep your email address.
  • If you upload a picture as your icon: a small copy of it (128×128 pixels).
  • Game information: the games you create or join, and the moves you make.

How it is stored

  • Guests (everyone who has not logged in with an account) are stored only in their browser's local storage, never on the server, and do not expire.
  • Accounts (username or Google/Microsoft) are saved in a database: the information above, plus when the account was created and last used. An account that is not used for 30 days is deleted automatically, together with its picture.
  • Games are kept only in the server's memory and are deleted when the server restarts.
  • Your browser stores a login token. For an account, it keeps you logged in for up to 30 days after your last visit, or until you log out. For a guest, it holds the guest's display name and icon, and lasts until you clear your browser's site data.
  • While you sign in with Google or Microsoft, a cookie is used for a few moments to complete the sign-in, and is then removed.

Sharing

Your information is not sold or shared with anyone, and is not used for advertising. There are no analytics or tracking tools.

The site is hosted by Render, the database by Neon, and the page loads a script from the jsDelivr content network. Like any web service, these providers may process your IP address to deliver the site.

Children

GamesLane is meant for family use. Accounts are optional; everyone starts as a guest and can play without providing any account information.

Contact

For questions or requests about your information, open an issue at github.com/inativ01/GamesLane2.0.
