Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

GamesLane is a free, non-commercial site for playing board and card games with friends and family. This page explains what information it uses and why.

Information we use

  • Your player name, which you type in. It is shown to the other people in your game.
  • If you sign in with Google or Microsoft: your account ID, name and email address, as provided by that service. They are used only to recognise you, so you can rejoin your games, and to fill in your player name. GamesLane never sees your Google or Microsoft password.
  • Game information: the games you create or join, and the moves you make.

How it is stored

  • Games are kept only in the server's memory and are deleted when the server restarts. No database is used.
  • If you sign in, a cookie keeps you signed in on that browser for up to 30 days, or until you sign out.
  • Your browser stores your player name and a random guest ID, so you can rejoin a game after refreshing the page. You can clear these by clearing your browser's site data.

Sharing

Your information is not sold or shared with anyone, and is not used for advertising. There are no analytics or tracking tools.

The site is hosted by Render, and the page loads a script from the jsDelivr content network. Like any web service, these providers may process your IP address to deliver the site.

Children

GamesLane is meant for family use. Signing in is optional; children can play as guests without providing any account information.

Contact

For questions or requests about your information, open an issue at github.com/inativ01/GamesLane2.0.
